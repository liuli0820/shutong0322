一炷香

yī zhù xiāng · one stick of incense

静

30:00

Unlit. One stick of incense is 30 minutes. Light it when you are ready to start.

Measure of time

Old Chinese clocks counted in kè and shíchen. From 1645 a day held 96 kè of fifteen minutes and 12 shíchen of two hours. A cup of tea and a stick of incense were looser, everyday measures.

Space light or pause · R reset · 1–4 measure
